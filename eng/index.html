Google Ads FAQ

Short answers to the most common questions about price, budget and working together.

How much does Google Ads management cost?

There are two parts: the ad budget, which you pay directly to Google, and our management fee. Management is €300, €500 or €750/month (+ VAT) depending on the package, and the ad budget must be at least €500/month. The packages differ in the number of campaigns, the depth of tracking and how often we optimise and report.

What is a sensible ad budget?

The minimum is €500/month, and we recommend starting at €1,000. In Finland a click costs €2–5 in many industries, and with a budget below €500 there are so few clicks that Google's algorithm cannot learn what works and the results are not reliable. In the free consultation we check the click prices in your industry and tell you what budget makes sense.

How quickly will I see results from Google Ads?

Ads usually appear in search results the same or next day after launch, and the first enquiries can come right away. The campaign improves as data builds up, so you typically get a reliable picture of results within 4–8 weeks.

What does the monthly fee include?

Every package includes building campaigns or cleaning up an existing account, keyword and negative keyword management, ad copy, a conversion tracking check, ongoing optimisation and a report on where the money went and what it brought in. Larger packages add more campaigns, more thorough tracking and more frequent optimisation and reporting.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. The account, its history and all its data stay yours, including after we stop working together. We only get access rights, which you can remove at any time.

Is there a fixed-term contract?

No. The agreement runs until further notice with one month's notice. You stay because the ads pay off, not because a contract holds you.

Can you take over an existing Google Ads account?

Yes. We first review the current campaigns, search terms and conversion tracking and show you where the budget is being wasted. Cutting irrelevant searches often brings clearly more enquiries for the same money.

How do I know whether the ads are working?

We make sure conversion tracking measures the right things, like calls, form submissions and quote requests, not just clicks. The report shows what each enquiry cost and how the numbers are developing.

Do I need a new website for Google Ads?

Not necessarily. If your current site clearly says what you do and makes it easy to get in touch, ads work well with it. If it loses the visitors the ads bring in, we can build a new site designed to support the ads.

Google Ads or SEO, which is better?

Google Ads brings visibility immediately and can be adjusted weekly, while SEO results build up over months. For many businesses Google Ads is the fastest way to more enquiries, and the search data from ads also helps with SEO later.
