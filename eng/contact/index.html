Don't want to write yourself? Request a free advertising assessment and we'll tell you how your company should advertise.

What should you say in your first message?

A few lines are enough. With this information we can get straight to the point:

  • What your company does and in which area
  • Whether you already have a Google Ads account, and who manages it now
  • Roughly how much has been or could be spent on advertising per month
  • What the biggest problem is now: no enquiries, cost is too high or tracking doesn't work

What happens next?

We arrange a short conversation where we go through your situation and tell you plainly whether Google Ads makes sense for you. The conversation is free and doesn't commit you to anything. If you want to continue, we send a quote, and the notice period for the agreement is one month.
